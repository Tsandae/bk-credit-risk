# Public trust statement

**What this is.** A research prototype of a credit-risk decision-support model. It is not used to approve or decline anyone for a loan.

**What it does.** It estimates the chance that a loan applicant will not repay, groups applicants into lower, medium and higher risk, and shows the main reasons for each estimate.

**What we tell you honestly.**
- It was built on public US loan data, not on Bank of Kigali customers. Its numbers do not describe Bank of Kigali borrowers.
- It is moderately accurate. Where it was tested, it found about 36% of the loans that went on to default, and it flagged many loans that were repaid.
- Lower-income applicants were flagged more often. We tested a fix; it works, but it roughly doubles the number of applications flagged for review and raises policy and legal questions, so it has not been adopted.
- Explanations show patterns the model uses. They are not proof of cause.

**What we promise.**
1. A person, not the model, makes every credit decision, and can override the model with a recorded reason.
2. Anyone affected can ask for the reasons behind a score and receive them in plain language.
3. We monitor fairness and accuracy continuously, publish what we find, and pause the model if limits are breached.
4. We protect personal data and keep only what is needed, for as long as needed.
5. We will say so, and fix it, when something goes wrong.
